# Spell FX audit (old client tables vs 0.36c)

## Summary (2026-10-15) - flagged, nothing changed
Checked: every client effect the server's spells use, against the old client's spells.csv / speffects.csv /
anims.csv / cc_spells.csv and what 0.36c built (spell_fx.json, the converted effect models, the sound table).

Clean: every effect model converted with its textures, every spell sound resolves to a file, every sound set is mapped.

Worth a look, biggest first:
1. **Radius effects are never played** (336 spell effects): spells.csv column 15, the effect around the target area of
   an area spell (e.g. Cloud of Rot's poison cloud, the shroud spells' red shock splash). SpellFx plays the target,
   buildup, projectile, resist and caster effects but not this one - area spells look smaller than in the old client.
2. **Pulse effects are never played** (85): column 13, the effect on each pulse / tick (e.g. Bones to Grit's
   Fleshbane hit on every DoT tick).
3. **Casting animations with no clip** (238 rows, mostly later-era sets): Warlock Gather / Cast, archery
   "Spell Version" bow clips and Volley, plus 1.65 ones like 67 EM Victory - those spells cast with the plain cast
   clips. Four taunt gestures name the Warlock Start clip, which no race figure has.
4. **Additive layers with a mask in the dark slot** (20 effect models, e.g. Holy Shield 2, Heat Sphere, Frost Spray,
   Paladin group heal): drawn as the full flat shape instead of the soft masked glow - the Bark Skin problem; only
   alb_holyshld1_hit is fixed (owner: no broad changes).
5. **Dark lit cut-out / blend layers** (74 effect models, e.g. the earth crack's mud, Arawn's eyes): the Strength of
   the Oak problem (dark ambient, white diffuse) - may look near black; only hib_oakleaves_hit is fixed.
6. Smaller: Play Anims column (44 effects) and Remove Effect on Cast (1) not read; 7 client effects the server uses
   don't exist in the client's spells.csv (savage style buffs, two procs, a taunt) - nothing can show for them;
   5 Volley effects name the client's "Empty Nif for Archery" (nothing to draw - as the client).

Details per section below.

Generated by pipeline/audit_spell_fx.py. 3165 client effects used by the server's spells were checked. Nothing was changed - these are flags to look at. "e.g." lists are cut to the first entries.
